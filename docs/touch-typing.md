# Touch Typing Guide

Touch typing means typing while relying on learned finger positions rather than visually searching for every key.

## Start with the home row

Common QWERTY home-row positions are:

- Left hand: A S D F
- Right hand: J K L ;

The F and J keys commonly have tactile markers that help you orient your hands.

## Practice principles

- Keep your hands relaxed.
- Use the intended finger consistently.
- Look at the screen instead of the keyboard when possible.
- Build accuracy before chasing speed.
- Practice difficult key combinations separately.
- Take short breaks during long sessions.
